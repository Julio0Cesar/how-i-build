This started as a problem with two bad answers.

Most of what I build lives in private repositories — some of it because it is
not mine to open, some because opening it gives away the thing itself. A private
repository proves nothing to anyone outside it. Opening everything solves the
proof and destroys the asset.

## What is public here

The decisions, not the code.

A project's write-up is what I hit, what I chose, what it cost, and what I would
do differently. That survives without the source, and it is the part that is
actually hard. The repository can stay closed and the reasoning can still be read.

## Why it reads like a notebook

Because it is one. Short entries, honest about trade-offs, with a changelog when
something genuinely changes. Not a tutorial site, not a wiki — those promise
completeness, and completeness is a promise nobody keeps.

The first project documented here is this site, which is the only honest place to
start: if I am going to claim that recording decisions is worth the effort, the
site making the claim should be the first thing to do it.

## Two languages

English is the default because that is where the readers are. Portuguese exists
because it is mine. Every case and every post exists in both — enforced by the
build, not by discipline.
