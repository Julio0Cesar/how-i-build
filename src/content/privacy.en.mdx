This site has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party embeds. Nothing
you do here is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Two things are stored in your browser, both only when you ask for them:

- **`NEXT_LOCALE`**, a cookie written when you choose a language with the EN | PT
  switch, so the next visit opens in the language you picked.
- **`theme`**, a `localStorage` entry written when you choose light, dark or
  system, so the page does not open in the wrong one.

Neither is read by anyone but this site, and neither identifies you. If you
never touch those two controls, nothing is written at all.

## Removing them

Clearing site data in your browser removes both. In Chrome and Edge that is
under Settings → Privacy → Cookies and site data; in Firefox, Settings →
Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data; in Safari, Settings → Privacy →
Manage Website Data.

## Hosting

Pages are served by [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), which
